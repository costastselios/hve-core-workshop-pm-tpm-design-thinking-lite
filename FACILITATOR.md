# Facilitator notes (v6.6)

How to use this material to run the workshop. Participants don't need this page.

## What's here

- **[README.md](README.md):** the participant guide. Participants keep it open in a browser tab all day.
- **[HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.6.html](HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.6.html):** the slide deck, with speaker notes. It's one file and works offline.
- **[Pack-A-after-Step-3.zip](Pack-A-after-Step-3.zip)** and **[Pack-D-after-Step-8.zip](Pack-D-after-Step-8.zip):** saved runs that everyone loads, so all pairs continue from the same starting point.

## Opening the deck

- **Online:** [costastselios.github.io/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.6.html](https://costastselios.github.io/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.6.html)
- **Offline:** download the file and open it in Chrome or Edge. It needs no network.
- The speaker notes are public: anyone who opens the deck can read them.

## Using the deck

- **Presenter view:** click **Presenter view** in the bottom bar and drag the new window to your laptop screen. It shows your notes, the next slide, a clock and an elapsed timer. Both windows move together.
- **Full screen:** press **F** in the projector window.
- **Keys:** ← / → (or Space, Page Up/Down) to move · **O** slide index · **N** notes · **?** all keys.
- **Dark theme:** a button in the bottom bar. Light is the default and suits most projectors.

## Shape of the day

Six hours. Times are elapsed from the start, so it works for any start time.

| Start | Section | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Introduction to the workshop and problem domain (facilitator) | 40 min |
| 0:40 | Step 0 · Look at three broker apps (hands-on) | 20 min |
| 1:00 | ◆ Regroup 1 | 10 min |
| 1:10 | Step 1 · First conversation with DT Coach (hands-on) | 30 min |
| 1:40 | Break (10 min) | 10 min |
| 1:50 | What two investors told us (facilitator) · everyone loads Pack A | 20 min |
| 2:10 | Step 4 · Brainstorm (hands-on) | 25 min |
| 2:35 | ◆ Regroup 2 | 10 min |
| 2:45 | Lunch (45 min) | 45 min |
| 3:30 | The shared product idea (facilitator) | 10 min |
| 3:40 | Step 6 · Test a prototype by hand (hands-on) | 35 min |
| 4:15 | ◆ Regroup 3 | 15 min |
| 4:30 | Break (10 min) | 10 min |
| 4:40 | Everyone loads Pack D · guided read of the BRD and PRD (facilitator reads along) | 25 min |
| 5:05 | Step 9 · Turn requirements into a plan (hands-on) | 20 min |
| 5:25 | Step 10 · Look back · ◆ Regroup 4 | 20 min |
| 5:45 | Close and Q&A (ends at 6:00) | 15 min |

- **Hands-on (pairs):** Steps 0, 1, 4, 6 and 9, plus loading the packs.
- **Facilitator-led:** the introduction, what two investors told us, the shared product idea and the guided read. These stand in for the steps that today's format skips.
- **◆ Regroups:** questions to the whole room, answered aloud. Start them on time.

## Before the day

- Send participants the guide link: [github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite).
- Ask them to complete the [prerequisites](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-pm-tpm-design-thinking-workshop/blob/main/docs/PREREQUISITES.md) and clone the [workshop repository](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-pm-tpm-design-thinking-workshop).
- On the room's Wi-Fi, check that the guide page, the deck and both zip downloads open.
- Keep both zips and the deck file on your laptop.

## Running the day

- **Pairs:** a driver types and clicks; an assistant reads the guide aloud. They swap at every break.
- **Packs:** everyone loads the same pack at each jump, even pairs who finished the step before it.
- **When you demo the drag:** say "choose **Copy Folder**" and "if VS Code asks **Replace?**, click **Cancel**".
- **Typical agent waits:** about 40 s for the Step 1 closing prompt, 70–95 s for the brainstorm, about 2 min for Step 9. Tell the room before they paste.
- **If the coach offers a second brainstorm round:** pairs paste the fallback prompt from the guide's Step 4.
- **Off-topic questions:** "Note it and raise it in the closing Q&A."

## If something breaks

- **A pair falls behind:** they load the next pack with everyone else and carry on.
- **GitHub or Wi-Fi is down:** share the zips and the deck from your laptop (USB stick or a Teams chat).
- **Windows:** the terminal fallback for loading packs hasn't been checked on real Windows. Prefer drag and drop.

## Using the packs to shape your own version

If you fork this workshop and want to skip some Design Thinking methods or steps, each pack is a saved, agent-generated state you can load to jump past them. Load the pack for the point you want to start from, and the agents carry on from there.

| Pack | Saved state after | Lets you skip | Download |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Step 3 | Steps 1–3 (DT Methods 1–3: scope, research, synthesis). DT Coach resumes at Method 4, the brainstorm. | [Pack-A-after-Step-3.zip](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/raw/main/Pack-A-after-Step-3.zip) |
| B | Step 6 | The brainstorm, concept and prototype (Methods 4–6). Includes the DT→BRD handoff for BRD Builder (Step 7). | [Pack-B-after-Step-6.zip](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/raw/main/Pack-B-after-Step-6.zip) |
| C | Step 7 | The BRD: an approved BRD, so you start at the PRD (Step 8) with PRD Builder. | [Pack-C-after-Step-7.zip](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/raw/main/Pack-C-after-Step-7.zip) |
| D | Step 8 | The BRD and PRD: both approved, so you start at Step 9 (Epics and Features). | [Pack-D-after-Step-8.zip](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/raw/main/Pack-D-after-Step-8.zip) |
| E | Step 9 | Everything up to the backlog: a finished Epic/Feature proposal, for comparison or to start straight at the Step 10 review. | [Pack-E-after-Step-9.zip](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-pm-tpm-design-thinking-lite/raw/main/Pack-E-after-Step-9.zip) |

A and D load by drag and drop. B, C and E use the older layout with a hidden `.copilot-tracking` folder; see the README inside each zip.

### Why this version uses only A and D

- **One day, about six hours.** The full workshop takes a newcomer far longer: the dry run measured 5.5–8 hours hands-on, before any regroups.
- **The moments with the biggest learning impact stay hands-on:**
  - Step 1: questioning a vague idea with DT Coach.
  - Step 4: brainstorming, seeing ideas grouped, then asking "did anyone actually ask for this?".
  - Step 6: the prototype test, where people finish the task without understanding what it did.
  - Step 9: turning requirements into Epics and Features with their open gates.
- **The facilitator shows the rest** briefly from the saved run: research and synthesis, the shared concept, and the BRD and PRD. That keeps the "what we skipped, what the coach did, what came out" story without long agent conversations.
- **Everyone loads the same pack at each jump,** so all pairs start each part from the same place, regroups compare like with like, and nobody needs catching up.
- **Two jumps are the minimum** that keeps the day's flow from idea to backlog intact.
