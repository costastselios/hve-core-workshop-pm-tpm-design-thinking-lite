# Facilitator notes (v6.5)

How to use this material to run the workshop. Participants don't need this page.

## What's here

- **[README.md](README.md):** the participant guide. Participants keep it open in a browser tab all day.
- **[HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.5.html](HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.5.html):** the slide deck, with speaker notes. It's one file and works offline.
- **[Pack-A-after-Step-3.zip](Pack-A-after-Step-3.zip)** and **[Pack-D-after-Step-8.zip](Pack-D-after-Step-8.zip):** saved runs that everyone loads, so all pairs continue from the same starting point.

## Opening the deck

- **Online:** [costastselios.github.io/hve-core-workshop-lite-participant-guide/HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.5.html](https://costastselios.github.io/hve-core-workshop-lite-participant-guide/HVE-Workshop-Facilitator-Deck-v6.5.html)
- **Offline:** download the file and open it in Chrome or Edge. It needs no network.
- The speaker notes are public: anyone who opens the deck can read them.

## Using the deck

- **Presenter view:** click **Presenter view** in the bottom bar and drag the new window to your laptop screen. It shows your notes, the next slide, a clock and an elapsed timer. Both windows move together.
- **Full screen:** press **F** in the projector window.
- **Keys:** ← / → (or Space, Page Up/Down) to move · **O** slide index · **N** notes · **?** all keys.
- **Dark theme:** a button in the bottom bar. Light is the default and suits most projectors.

## Shape of the day

Six hours. Times are elapsed from the start, so it works for any start time.

| Start | Section | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Introduction to the workshop and problem domain (facilitator) | 40 min |
| 0:40 | Step 0 · Look at three broker apps (hands-on) | 20 min |
| 1:00 | ◆ Regroup 1 | 10 min |
| 1:10 | Step 1 · First conversation with DT Coach (hands-on) | 30 min |
| 1:40 | Break (10 min) | 10 min |
| 1:50 | What two investors told us (facilitator) · everyone loads Pack A | 20 min |
| 2:10 | Step 4 · Brainstorm (hands-on) | 25 min |
| 2:35 | ◆ Regroup 2 | 10 min |
| 2:45 | Lunch (45 min) | 45 min |
| 3:30 | The shared product idea (facilitator) | 10 min |
| 3:40 | Step 6 · Test a prototype by hand (hands-on) | 35 min |
| 4:15 | ◆ Regroup 3 | 15 min |
| 4:30 | Break (10 min) | 10 min |
| 4:40 | Everyone loads Pack D · guided read of the BRD and PRD (facilitator reads along) | 25 min |
| 5:05 | Step 9 · Turn requirements into a plan (hands-on) | 20 min |
| 5:25 | Step 10 · Look back · ◆ Regroup 4 | 20 min |
| 5:45 | Close and Q&A (ends at 6:00) | 15 min |

- **Hands-on (pairs):** Steps 0, 1, 4, 6 and 9, plus loading the packs.
- **Facilitator-led:** the introduction, what two investors told us, the shared product idea and the guided read. These stand in for the steps that today's format skips.
- **◆ Regroups:** questions to the whole room, answered aloud. Start them on time.

## Before the day

- Send participants the guide link: [github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-lite-participant-guide](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-workshop-lite-participant-guide).
- Ask them to complete the [prerequisites](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-pm-tpm-design-thinking-workshop/blob/main/docs/PREREQUISITES.md) and clone the [workshop repository](https://github.com/costastselios/hve-core-pm-tpm-design-thinking-workshop).
- On the room's Wi-Fi, check that the guide page, the deck and both zip downloads open.
- Keep both zips and the deck file on your laptop.

## Running the day

- **Pairs:** a driver types and clicks; an assistant reads the guide aloud. They swap at every break.
- **Packs:** everyone loads the same pack at each jump, even pairs who finished the step before it.
- **When you demo the drag:** say "choose **Copy Folder**" and "if VS Code asks **Replace?**, click **Cancel**".
- **Typical agent waits:** about 40 s for the Step 1 closing prompt, 70–95 s for the brainstorm, about 2 min for Step 9. Tell the room before they paste.
- **If the coach offers a second brainstorm round:** pairs paste the fallback prompt from the guide's Step 4.
- **Off-topic questions:** "Note it and raise it in the closing Q&A."

## If something breaks

- **A pair falls behind:** they load the next pack with everyone else and carry on.
- **GitHub or Wi-Fi is down:** share the zips and the deck from your laptop (USB stick or a Teams chat).
- **Windows:** the terminal fallback for loading packs hasn't been checked on real Windows. Prefer drag and drop.
